# Lighting Look 2.20.1 — game color pipeline, scenario sky, new sidebar

The Lighting Look no longer needs an FModel dump and no longer uses hand-tuned
pairings (`assets/lighting_looks.json`, saturation/hue tweaks, the 227.52°
HDRI rotation, Flip X). Everything comes from the game files.

## Data

`assets/lighting_scenarios.json` (530 KB, build 26159202) — 61 raid scenarios
on 7 maps + 4 Raider Den setups, each with its unbound post-process volume,
sun/moon, sky light, sky-actor cloud layer, SkyAtmosphere and height fog, plus
49 exposure bias curves. Generated by `scripts/lighting/` (see its README).
LUT strips, cloud textures and the exposure meter mask are extracted on demand
from the user's game install (bundled ArcPak, PNG); an FModel dump is used when
it already has them. All 15 LUT strips extract bit-identical to the raw texels.

## Color (compositor group `ArcLightingLook`, stack v12)

Order matches the game's tonemapper (consumer shader `10EE0D70…`):

1. **Exposure** — steady-state eye adaptation. Weighted log-average of
   mean(RGB) (meter mask `T_ExposureMeter_Mask_01` when the scenario uses it),
   white point = average / 0.18 clamped to `1.2·2^Min/MaxBrightness`, bias +
   `AutoExposureBiasCurve(EV100 of the white point)`. "Locked meter" feeds a
   fixed EV100 through the same math.
2. **Bloom** — Glare (Bloom) added at `BloomIntensity · Σtint / 6`.
3. **Vignette** — `cos⁴` mask in aspect-corrected circle space × `VignetteIntensity`.
4. **Shaper** — `q = saturate(log2(H + 0.0026677) / 14 + 0.6107269)`.
5. **LUT** — 32³ LUT from `lighting_grade` (port of the combine-LUTs shader:
   white balance, AP1 grading wheels, expand gamut, blue correction, Embark
   tone operator, authored strip blend, scene color tint), trilinear through two
   bilinear Map UV samples + blue lerp, `×1.05`, clamp, sRGB decode; the
   Standard view re-encodes.

Blender pixel values are treated as Unreal nits: a sun lamp of strength S
lights a white Lambertian surface to S/π, the same as S lux in Unreal.

## Sky & sun

- Physical sky (Multiple Scattering) rescaled per channel so its
  top-of-atmosphere sun equals the scenario light (lux × color × temperature);
  calibrated constants in `lighting_sky.py` (Blender 5.1.2).
- Camera rays see the sky (sun disc on); other rays see it × SkyLight
  intensity × color, like Unreal's captured sky light.
- Sun (or moon at night) lamp: exact world direction (sky actor root yaw ×
  light rotation), color with temperature, strength = lux × model
  transmittance at that elevation, angle = LightSourceAngle.
- The scenario's cloud layer (`Cloud Wisps Texture` × opacity) over the sky,
  zenith-centered polar mapping.

## Known approximations / gaps

- Bloom is single-scale (game: six Gaussian stages).
- Local exposure (scenario `LocalExposure*` contrast scales) is not applied (added in 2.20.2, see `lighting-local-exposure-2.20.2.md`).
- Exposure: the C++ side of eye adaptation (white-point clamp, curve input in
  white-point EV100, mean-RGB luminance) follows Unreal 5 behaviour, not a
  shader decode; the meter is computed at render resolution.
- Cloud layer projection and brightness approximate the Ultra Dynamic Sky
  material (the photo textures put the horizon near 0.9 of the radius). The
  rotation slider exists because the game animates the layer. *Superseded in
  2.20.3: the sky sphere shader and mesh UV are decoded, see
  [lighting-look-2.20.3.md](lighting-look-2.20.3.md).*
- `hdri_sky_072_8K_Sphere` and `T_CloudsCirrus_01_M` fail in the bundled
  extractor (duplicate package entries); those scenarios fall back to a clear
  sky and say so. *Fixed in 2.20.4: the bundled extractor is rebuilt with the
  duplicate-path lookup ([pak-index-duplicate-paths.md](pak-index-duplicate-paths.md))
  and the fixed project root ([pak-extract-mount-root.md](pak-extract-mount-root.md));
  both textures extract under `PioneerGame/Content`.*
- Volumetric clouds, stars, the moon disc and lens effects are not emulated.
